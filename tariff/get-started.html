Your Action Plan

Here's exactly what you need to do, in order, to claim your refund:

📅 Week 1 (This Week)

1

Verify Form 5106

Contact your customs broker to confirm your Form 5106 is on file and has current email address. This is the email where CBP sends your ACE Portal verification code.

CBP.gov: Directory of Licensed Customs Brokers - find your broker or verify your current broker's license status.

2

Apply for ACE Portal

Use the automated application at cbp.gov/portal-applying. Click "Importers" in Step 3.

3

Pull Entry Data

Request ES-701/ES-702 reports from your broker for all entries with IEEPA tariffs. Identify which entries are unliquidated vs. liquidated and when.

📅 Week 2-3

4

Complete ACE Setup

Once your ACE Portal account is approved, set up Importer sub-account view for each IOR number.

5

Enroll in ACH

Add your U.S. bank account information in the ACE Portal's ACH Refund Authorization tab. This is mandatory for receiving refunds.

6

Prepare & File CAPE Declaration

Generate CSV file with entry numbers for Phase 1 eligible entries. Upload through the CAPE tab in ACE Portal.

📅 Ongoing

7

Monitor Status

Track through REV-603 and REV-615 reports in ACE. Monitor for ACH deposits within 60-90 days.

8

File Protests

For entries approaching the 180-day deadline that aren't covered by Phase 1, file a protest to preserve your refund rights.

Before You Begin

Gather these items before starting the refund process:

📄 Required Documents

  • CBP Form 5106 (Importer Identity Form) — confirm it's on file
  • EIN / Tax ID number
  • Importer of Record (IOR) number(s)
  • Entry summaries (CBP Form 7501) for all IEEPA-tariff entries
  • ES-701/ES-702 reports from ACE

🏦 Required Information

  • U.S. bank account with ABA routing number
  • Current email address (must match Form 5106 POC email)
  • Company legal name and address
  • Customs broker contact information
  • List of all entry numbers subject to IEEPA tariffs
