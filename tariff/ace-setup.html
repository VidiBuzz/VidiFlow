ACE Portal Setup Guide

Complete step-by-step instructions for setting up your ACE Portal account and ACH refund enrollment — required before you can file a CAPE Declaration.

What is ACE?

"The Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) is the United States's centralized digital system for processing imports and exports. ACE is also known as the U.S. 'Single Window' because it provides a single, centralized access point connecting CBP, Partner Government Agencies and the business community." — U.S. Customs and Border Protection

ACE is CBP's centralized digital system for processing all imports and exports. The ACE Secure Data Portal (ACE Portal) is the web-based interface where importers log in to access their data, file entries, submit protests, request refunds, and run reports.

💻

ACE Portal

The web-based access point connecting CBP, the trade community, and Partner Government Agencies. Provides real-time access to trade transactions, account management, and trade data through ACE Reports.

🔒

ACE Secure Data Portal

A centralized online access point for the trade community to connect with CBP and government agencies involved in importing goods. Eliminates redundancy, reduces paper usage, and facilitates faster processing.

📊

ACE Reports

A tool providing access to a wide range of data. Users can run targeted reports to conduct in-house audits, identify systemic errors, and gain insight into entries under review by CBP.

ACE Account Architecture

ACE Portal accounts have a three-layer structure. Understanding this is critical for proper setup.

🏢 Layer 1: Top Account

The company-level master account. One per company/organization. Contains all sub-accounts and user management. Created when you first apply for ACE Portal access.

📄 Layer 2: Sub-Accounts

Importer Sub-Account View is REQUIRED for CAPE filings and ACH refunds. Each Importer of Record (IOR) number needs its own importer sub-account. Also available: Exporter, Protest Filer, Broker sub-accounts.

👤 Layer 3: User Profiles

Trade Account Owner (TAO) = Primary administrator. Proxy TAO (PTAO) = Delegated admin rights. Trade Account User (TAU) = Regular user with specific permissions.

Step-by-Step Setup Process

1

Prerequisite: CBP Form 5106 Must Be On File

The Create/Update Importer Identity Form (CBP Form 5106) establishes your company as an Importer of Record with CBP. This is required BEFORE ACE account creation.

Information Required: Full legal business name, physical address, EIN/Tax ID, ownership structure, primary contact name/phone/EMAIL, business activity description, types of products to import.

Submit through: Your customs broker (recommended), self-file via ACE Portal, or in person at CBP port of entry.

CBP.gov: Form 5106 - Create/Update Importer Identity Form. This form MUST be on file before you can apply for an ACE Portal account.

CBP Form 5106 (PDF): The official form showing all required fields including business name, address, EIN, and primary contact email.

⚠ CRITICAL: The email address on your Form 5106 is where CBP sends the ACE Portal verification code. If the email on your 5106 is an old broker's email, you CANNOT receive the verification code and cannot complete account creation. Contact your broker to update the 5106 email to your current email FIRST.
2

Apply for ACE Portal Account (Automated Process)

Eligibility: Must have existing CBP Form 5106 on file. Must NOT already have ACE Portal top account. Email used must not be tied to existing ACE account.

Application Link: https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/how-to-use-ace/portal-applying (Click "Importers" link in Step 3: Apply)

Process: Enter Company Name, Importer Record Number (IR#), and Requester's email. System validates that email is not associated with existing ACE account, IR# matches existing 5106 record, and POC email on 5106 is current. Verification code is sent to POC email on Form 5106. Enter code and complete application. Wait for CBP approval (5-10 business days).

CBP.gov: ACE Portal Application page. Click the "Importers" link in Step 3 to begin the automated application process.

CBP ACE Portal Account Application Guide (PDF): Official step-by-step instructions from CBP for completing the application.

3

Get Importer Sub-Account View

If you already have an ACE account but no Importer View, you need to request sub-account access.

Method A (Automated): Log into existing ACE Portal top account. Navigate to Tools → Manage Sub-Accounts. Request Importer Sub-Account View. Provide Importer of Record (IOR) numbers.

Method B (Manual): Submit request letter on company letterhead, signed by TAO or authorized company official. Email to: ACE.Applications@cbp.dhs.gov. Must include company name, address, IOR numbers exactly as originally filed. Processing time: 5-10 business days.

4

Enroll in ACH Refunds (MANDATORY)

Effective February 6, 2026: CBP issues ALL refunds via ACH electronic payment only. NO PAPER CHECKS.

Prerequisites: ACE Portal account with Importer sub-account view. U.S. bank account with U.S. ABA routing number. Foreign banks need U.S. correspondent bank.

Enrollment Process: Log into ACE Portal as TAO or authorized user. Navigate to Importer sub-account view. Select "ACH Refund Authorization" tab. Click "Get Info/Refresh" to check if bank info exists. If no info: Click "Add ACH Info". Enter: Bank name, Account number, Routing number (ABA), Account type. Submit for CBP approval (2-3 business days).

Multiple IOR Numbers: Need separate ACH enrollment for EACH IOR number, even if same bank account.

CBP.gov: ACE Portal and ACH Refunds page. This is where you enroll your bank account to receive electronic refunds.

CBP ACH Enrollment Guide (PDF): Official instructions for enrolling in electronic refunds through the ACE Portal.

CBP ACH Bank Information for Electronic Refunds (PDF): Shows exactly what bank information you need to provide.

CBP.gov: ACE Portal and ACH Refunds FAQs - answers common questions about the enrollment process.

CBP ACH FAQ Expanded: Detailed answers about refund timelines, multiple IOR numbers, and bank account requirements.

5

Grant User Access to ACH Tab

As TAO, authorize other users to access the ACH Refund Authorization tab: ACE Portal home → Tools tab. In User Access pane, select user from Search Results. Select Roles tab → Expand Importer sub-account title. Click Edit. In ACH Refund tab row → Select "Full Access" radio button. Save.
