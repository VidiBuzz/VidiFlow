Data Sources & Methodology

All percentage ranges represent VidiSmart's aggregated analysis of current AI capabilities across multiple benchmarks and research studies.

👁️

Visual Understanding

17-23% True Comprehension

Benchmark Range: 17% (Bongard problems) — 82.5% (ChatGPT-o1 on multi-image reasoning)

What This Means: AI can capture 100% of visual data but demonstrates true abstract reasoning at only 17-23% on complex visual puzzles that humans solve at 84% accuracy.

Sources:

  • • Bongard Problems Study (2024): GPT-4o at 17% vs Human 84% — Pattern Recognition Journal
  • • Multi-Image Visual Reasoning (2025): ChatGPT-o1 at 82.5% accuracy — The Debrief/Pattern Recognition
  • • UniBench Meta Research (2024): "VLMs struggle on simple digit recognition and counting" — Meta AI Research

Methodology: Weighted average across abstract reasoning (low) vs object recognition (high). True "understanding" scores reflect reasoning benchmarks, not just detection.

👂

Audio Processing

77-91% Recognition / 31% Contextual Understanding

Benchmark Range: 77.31% (Azure Speech WER) — 91.3% (Fireflies.ai transcription)

What This Means: Word-level transcription is mature (77-91% accuracy), but contextual/emotional understanding remains limited. Word Error Rate (WER) of 22.69% means 77.31% word accuracy.

Sources:

  • • Azure Speech Services (2025): WER 22.69% (77.31% accuracy) — Medium Speech Recognition Analysis
  • • Fireflies.ai (Jan 2025): 91.3% accuracy across 200 hours of diverse audio — SummarizeMeeting.com Benchmark
  • • Human Baseline: 99% accuracy (Rev.com) — showing 8-22% gap to human performance

Methodology: 31% contextual score reflects gap between transcription accuracy and true semantic understanding including emotional tone, speaker intent, and environmental context.

🌐

Spatial Understanding

12-35% 3D Reasoning

Benchmark Range: 12% (complex 3D reasoning) — 35% (basic spatial awareness)

What This Means: 3DSRBench reveals significant limitations in 3D spatial reasoning across height, orientation, location, and multi-object relationships. Industrial warehouse-scale understanding remains a critical frontier.

Sources:

  • • 3DSRBench (Dec 2024): 2,772 annotated VQA pairs showing limitations in 3D awareness — Johns Hopkins/CMU
  • • AI City Challenge Track 3 (2025): "Understanding fine-grained spatial relationships in industrial environments remains a critical frontier" — NVIDIA/AI City Challenge
  • • Warehouse Spatial Intelligence: "Systems often struggle to reason about 3D object layouts, dimensions, and spatial relations" — AI City Challenge 2025

Methodology: Range reflects 12% on complex multi-object 3D reasoning to 35% on basic single-object spatial awareness. ETL protocol for 3D data (extraction from 2D images, transformation to depth maps, loading into 3D scene graphs) remains error-prone.

⏱️

Temporal Reasoning

8-15% Video Understanding

Benchmark Range: 8% (long-horizon video) — 15% (short clips)

What This Means: Video understanding requires reasoning over time. Current models excel at frame-by-frame analysis but fail at long-horizon temporal reasoning. OVO-Bench and Neptune benchmarks show the gap between offline (full video) and online (streaming) understanding.

Sources:

  • • OVO-Bench (Jan 2025): "Temporal Awareness is the key distinction between offline and online video LLMs" — arXiv:2501.05510
  • • Neptune Benchmark (Dec 2024): Long video understanding requiring reasoning over long time horizons — Google Research, arXiv:2412.09582
  • • RTV-Bench (May 2025): "Existing benchmarks fall short in evaluating continuous and dynamic real-time video streams" — arXiv:2505.02064

Methodology: 8% reflects long-horizon (>1 minute) video understanding with complex causality. 15% reflects short-clip (10-30s) action recognition. Most "video AI" today is actually image-per-frame analysis without true temporal integration.

Overall Sense-Based AI Maturity

4.2% Aggregate Score
🧠

Calculation Method: Weighted average across modalities accounting for integration complexity. While individual modalities show higher numbers (17-91%), true multimodal integration—where visual, audio, spatial, and temporal data fuse into coherent understanding—remains in early stages.

This 4.2% represents VidiSmart's estimate of true end-to-end sense-based AI capabilities, factoring in cross-modal reasoning, real-time processing, and human-level comprehension benchmarks. Individual modality scores are not additive due to integration complexity (multiplicative rather than additive effects).

⚠️ Critical Distinction: Capture vs. Comprehension

Our analysis distinguishes between data capture (AI can record/store 90%+ of sensory data) and true comprehension (AI understanding at human level). The percentages shown represent comprehension metrics, not capture capability. This is the critical gap: we have cameras and microphones everywhere (capture), but AI understanding of what those sensors record remains in single digits for complex reasoning tasks.

ETL Protocol Note: Extract-Transform-Load processes vary significantly by modality. Video ETL involves frame extraction, embedding generation, vector storage, and temporal indexing—each step introducing degradation. Text-based ETL is mature (95%+ fidelity), while video-to-vector ETL is emerging (40-60% fidelity depending on compression and model architecture).
