## Comparison Checklist

For each tool, test and note:
- [ ] Registration process difficulty
- [ ] Free tier limits (credits, duration, watermarks)
- [ ] Video quality (resolution, motion smoothness)
- [ ] Generation speed
- [ ] Prompt understanding accuracy
- [ ] Image-to-video capability
- [ ] Text-to-video capability
- [ ] Download quality and format
- [ ] Commercial usage rights
