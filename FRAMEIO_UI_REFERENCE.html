Frame-Accurate Comments

The "Killer Feature". Comments are not just text; they are temporal data points anchored to a specific frame (timecode) and spatial coordinate (XY) on the video canvas.

Asset Management Specs

Thumbnail Intelligence

Thumbnails aren't static. They are live portals into the video content.

  • Hover Scrub: Moving cursor scrubs through video (GIF/WebP sprite).
  • Duration Badge: Bottom right pill (dark bg).
  • Status Indicator: Colored corner dot (Green = Approved).

Status Workflows

Moving assets from "Draft" to "Approved" triggers automated actions. Smart Channel CX utilizes a state-machine approach.

PENDING

Upload complete, processing proxies. Not ready for review.

IN REVIEW

Share links active, comments open. Iteration phase.

APPROVED

Locked for edits. Ready for download/export.

Review Links

Public-facing, brandable links that allow external clients to comment without an account. The "AirBnb" of video review.

Encryption

Password protection & expiration dates.

Permissions

Toggle download access or view-only.

VidiSmart // 2026 OBJECTIVE

The Browser-Native Editing Experience

We aren't just building a player; we're building a complete non-linear editor (NLE) right in the web browser. This interface merges the power of Remotion's programmatic engine with the intuitive drag-and-drop workflow of traditional editors.

Traditional NLE Stack

The core objective is to provide a familiar environment for professional editors while they work with AI-generated assets.

  • Media Folders (Left): Direct access to all RAW and AI-generated image/video folders.
  • Program Monitor (Center): High-fidelity real-time preview of the current edit state.
  • Multi-Track Timeline (Bottom): Granular control over the timing and layering of every asset.
