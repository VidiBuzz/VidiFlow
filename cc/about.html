Our Story

Building extraordinary hospitality experiences across the Caribbean and beyond for over three decades.

Mastering the Complexities of Island Resort Development

Founded and led by Managing Partner Douglas Welles, Caribbean Consultants Management, LLC brings over 30 years of elite expertise to the world's most challenging and prestigious hospitality projects. We don't just build resorts; we navigate the intricate logistics, stringent regulatory frameworks, and unique environmental demands of remote island development.

Doug's extraordinary portfolio spans four continents and over $2 billion in total project value. From managing the massive construction of the Royal Towers at Atlantis in the Bahamas, to overseeing the development of the ultra-luxury Ritz-Carlton Reserve Dorado Beach in Puerto Rico, our track record is defined by iconic, landmark destinations.

Whether acting as an Owner's Representative for The St. Regis Bali or driving the historic renovation of the Belmond Hotel Caruso in Italy, we protect our clients' interests with uncompromising integrity. We deliver world-class luxury experiences on time, on budget, and in perfect harmony with their breathtaking natural surroundings.
