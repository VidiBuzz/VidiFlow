### Service 1: Owner's Representation

**Overview:**
> As your dedicated owner's representative, we act as an extension of your team, protecting your interests throughout every phase of the project. We bridge the gap between ownership and execution, ensuring your vision becomes reality while maintaining budget, schedule, and quality control.
> 
> Whether you're an investor, developer, or high-net-worth individual, we provide the expertise and oversight you need - without requiring you to be on-site daily.

**Key Deliverables:**
- Budget management and cost control
- Schedule oversight and delay mitigation
- Quality assurance and inspection coordination
- Vendor and contractor selection/management
- Risk management and issue resolution

**Projects:**
- L'Ermitage Beverly Hills - Complete hotel transformation
- Hyatt Regency Kauai - Multi-year renovation
- St. Regis Fort Lauderdale - Oceanfront tower development

### Service 2: Luxury Resort Development

**Overview:**
> From undeveloped land to world-class destination, we manage the entire development lifecycle. Our comprehensive services cover master planning, entitlement, design oversight, and construction management - delivering turnkey resort projects ready for operation.

**Phases (5-step process):**

1. **Discovery** - Conceptual Design & Feasibility
   - Site analysis, market study, program development, conceptual design, budget estimating

2. **Planning** - Entitlement & Permitting
   - Zoning approvals, environmental permits, government coordination, community engagement

3. **Design** - Design Oversight
   - Architect selection, interior design coordination, landscape architecture, value engineering

4. **Construction** - Construction Management
   - Contractor selection, construction supervision, QA/QC, budget tracking, schedule management

5. **Completion** - Commissioning & Closeout
   - System testing, staff training, operational readiness, handover to management

### Service 3: Caribbean Hospitality Expertise

**Overview:**
> The Caribbean presents unique challenges and opportunities that demand specialized knowledge. With over 30 years operating throughout the region, we possess deep, practical understanding of island logistics, regulatory environments, sustainable development practices, and community dynamics.

**4 Key Areas:**
1. **Island Logistics** - Expert coordination of material shipping, equipment importation, supply chain management across island chains
2. **Regulatory Navigation** - Deep relationships with USVI, Caribbean territorial, and national regulatory bodies for efficient permitting
3. **Sustainable Development** - Expertise in hurricane-resistant construction, renewable energy integration, eco-friendly resort design
4. **Community Engagement** - Proven ability to work with local communities, create jobs, ensure projects benefit host islands
