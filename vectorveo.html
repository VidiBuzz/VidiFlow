Choosing Your Embedding Model

Industry-Leading Models: TwelveLabs Marengo & Pegasus

TwelveLabs has emerged as a leader in AI video intelligence with two flagship models that represent the cutting edge of video understanding technology.

Marengo 2.7: Multimodal Video Embeddings

Marengo is TwelveLabs' state-of-the-art foundation model for generating multimodal video embeddings—compact 1024-dimensional vector representations that capture the complete meaning of video content. Unlike traditional single-vector approaches, Marengo 2.7 uses a pioneering multi-vector embedding architecture that decomposes video into specialized vectors, achieving over 15% improvement in accuracy over its predecessor.

Built on a Transformer-based architecture, Marengo processes videos through a unified framework that understands visual elements (fine-grained object detection, motion dynamics, temporal relationships), audio elements (native speech, non-verbal sounds, music), and can even embed text and images into the same vector space for true cross-modality search.

Key Specifications:
  • • Embedding Dimension: 1024 vectors
  • • Similarity Metric: Cosine similarity
  • • Modalities: Video, audio, text, images
  • • Architecture: Multi-vector Transformer

Use Cases: Semantic video search, content recommendation, cross-modal retrieval, video clustering, duplicate detection, and establishing domain expertise for GEO optimization. Available on Amazon Bedrock in US East, Europe (Ireland), and Asia Pacific (Seoul).

Pegasus 1.2: Video-to-Text Generation

Pegasus is TwelveLabs' generative video language model designed for sophisticated video-to-text applications. Released in February 2025, Pegasus 1.2 represents an industry-grade breakthrough in long-form video understanding, capable of processing videos up to 1 hour in length while maintaining best-in-class accuracy with low latency.

The model excels at granular visual comprehension—detecting objects, reading on-screen text, interpreting numerical content, and providing precise temporal grounding to identify when events occur. Its multimodal understanding combines visual, audio, and textual signals to generate contextually rich text including summaries, highlights, detailed reports, creative content, and answers to complex questions about video content.

Key Capabilities:
  • • Max Video Length: Up to 1 hour
  • • Text Generation: Summaries, titles, reports, Q&A
  • • Temporal Grounding: Precise timestamp identification
  • • Multimodal Fusion: Visual + audio + text analysis

Use Cases: Automatic video summarization, marketing copy generation, content moderation, educational content extraction, accessibility (captions/transcripts), and creating rich Schema.org VideoObject metadata for AI Overviews and GEO. Available on Amazon Bedrock.

The Power of Combining Both Models

Use Marengo to create searchable embeddings of your entire video library, enabling semantic discovery. Then apply Pegasus to generate rich metadata, transcripts, and structured content that feeds AI search engines. Together, they create the complete "Visual AI Schema" that establishes your domain authority and dominates GEO results.

The GEO Flywheel: Creating a "Visual AI Schema"

The ultimate goal is to bridge the gap between your unstructured video content and the structured data AI models crave. By creating a "Visual AI Schema," you generate rich, machine-readable metadata that makes your videos exceptionally valuable to AI Overviews, creating a powerful, self-reinforcing flywheel for authority and traffic.

The Flywheel Effect

1

Analyze Video: Automatically extract transcripts, objects, and scenes from your video content.

2

Generate Rich Schema: Programmatically create detailed `Schema.org/VideoObject` data with chapters, keywords, and transcripts.

3

Feed the AI: AI search engines consume this rich data, understanding your content at a granular level.

4

Dominate Overviews: Your content is cited and featured in AI answers, establishing authority and driving engagement.

Case Study: Sourdough Video

A search for "sourdough baking steps" could generate an AI Overview that directly lists the video's chapters as steps, because the rich schema made that information available.

Market & Future Outlook

Understand the current data market and the future trajectory of multimodal AI. This section explains why custom embeddings are a competitive advantage and how today's work is a foundational investment in the future of AI-driven information discovery.

Build, Don't Buy

The market for pre-computed video embeddings is virtually non-existent because their true value lies in domain specificity. Generic embeddings are lossy and can't capture the nuances of your industry.

Creating custom embeddings from your own content builds a proprietary semantic space—a competitive moat that signals true expertise to AI models and cannot be easily replicated.

The Road Ahead

The field is moving towards AI that can natively reason across text, images, and video in real-time. Future search engines won't just link to your video; they may "watch" relevant segments to formulate an answer.

Building a deep, semantically searchable video library today is not just a GEO tactic; it's the creation of a foundational, "AI-native" asset for your organization's future.
