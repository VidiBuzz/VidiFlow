What VidiFlow Does

Three Capabilities. One Visual Layer.

Here's exactly what VidiFlow does, how it does it, and where we are with each capability right now.

🤖
Core Platform

VidiFlow: Conductor

LIVE

What it does: Blends Human Representation (HR) and Human Experience (HX) into a visual command plane. AgentGen dynamically instantiates specialized agents while Persona Matching aligns their voice, style, and behavior.

How it works: Processes user segments through a Personality Modeling Matrix to select emotional traits, formal style scales, and ElevenLabs/PlayHT voice clones. Telemetry streams via Kafka to feed the real-time visual grid.

Status: Live and operational — managing 127+ agent twins across projects
🔍
SmartGen Brand Discovery

VidiFlow: SmartChannel CX

ACTIVE

What it does: Autonomous agents scan market data, brand signals, and competitive intelligence to surface high-value brand partnership opportunities — visually, in real time.

How it works: Crawls 50+ data sources to score and plot targets on the dashboard. Employs content-aware smart media creation tools for VidiMail (video email), VidiBlast (campaigns), VidiTwin (clones), and VidiShop (visualize before you buy) alongside Personal AI Storytelling.

Status: Active discovery engine — scanning, scoring, and synthesizing daily
💰
Revenue Intelligence

VidiFlow: $$ Profit Flow

BUILDING

What it does: Maps every agent as a revenue node in a live cash flow topology. Shows where money is being generated, where it's leaking, and where agents can autonomously optimize for yield.

How it works: Financial data streams feed into the visual layer. Revenue streams pulse with intensity. Anomalies trigger amber alerts. Agents are authorized to rebalance resources toward highest-yield operations within pre-set guardrails.

Status: In active development — visual mapping live, autonomous optimization Q3 2026
