#10 The Reality Check: What AI Does NOT Do Well

Newcomers to AI are currently deluged with uncritical hype claiming AI will replace everything tomorrow. Setting false expectations causes costly corporate mistakes, hallucinated contracts, and broken customer trust.

At Vidi.News and VidiFlow, we believe true literacy requires being ruthlessly specific about what AI cannot do well — and where it actively fails:

RISK AUDIT The AI Failure Index: 6 Critical Boundaries
Vidi.News Investigation

BOUNDARY 01 // Hallucinations & Fake Veracity

LLMs generate statistically plausible-sounding sentences, not verified truth. Without a deterministic grounding database (RAG / Postgres), AI will invent fake legal citations, fictitious revenue numbers, and non-existent product features with supreme confidence.

BOUNDARY 02 // Deterministic Math & Financial Precision

AI predicts words; it does not calculate numbers. It cannot balance a GAAP general ledger, compute compound interest, or calculate manufacturing tolerances reliably without handing off the calculation to external Python or SQL code.

BOUNDARY 03 // Long-Horizon Autonomy & State Drift

Isolated agents drift off-track after 3 or 4 iterations. Without an architectural supervisor like VidiFlow keeping them on course, autonomous agents loop endlessly, forget their primary goal, or solve imaginary problems.

BOUNDARY 04 // Physical Reality & Real-Time Common Sense

AI has zero physical sensory grounding. It doesn't know if a pallet physically arrived at the loading dock, if a supplier went bankrupt this morning, or if a factory floor is flooded unless real-time IoT sensors and webhooks feed it.

BOUNDARY 05 // Human Empathy, Taste & Strategic Nuance
