1 — J0nasW/AI-Dictionary (16.6k AI phrases, MIT)

Whole-AI-domain phrase list with weights + embeddings. Best seed coverage; needs dedupe (e.g. duplicates like "graph neural networkand") and has no definitions — join to OpenGloss/Wiktionary for glosses.

2 — GIST multilingual-terminology (~5k AI terms, Apache-2.0)

Sourced from award papers across 18 top AI conferences, LLM-extracted + human-translated + GPT-4o-validated. Highest-quality AI term list; viewer currently broken (CSV per language); terms only.

3 — arxiv-acronym-gen (8,070 acronym→title)

Compact JSON (742kB). Ideal acronym→expansion shape for LEX, but arXiv science skew (MICE, LIGO) — little enterprise tech (CRM/SaaS/KPI). Use as expansion pattern + retrieval eval.

4 — OpenGloss v1.1 definitions (506k senses, CC-BY-4.0)

Synthetic encyclopedic dictionary: definition + synonyms/antonyms/hypernyms/hyponyms + collocations + examples + reading_level (K–PhD) + domain tags. Filter domain:technology for LEX one-liner drafts and connectsWith graph. Requires attribution.

5 — acronym_identification (AAAI-21 SDU)

17.5k manually annotated sentences, BIO scheme (B-short/I-short/B-long/I-long). For training the hover detector (which tokens are acronyms), not for shipped definitions.

6 — PLOD-filtered (160k+ abbreviation segments)

PLOS-journal abbreviation detection with RoBERTa/AlBERT baselines (F1 ~0.92). Same use as #5: detection model training.

7 — machine-learning-glossary-ai (AheadMint, curated)

Small curated benchmark: neural nets, transformers, RAG, embeddings, MLOps with math + engineering refs. Good editorial reference for AI-core LEX entries.

8 — ai-infrastructure-economics-glossary (11 terms, CC-BY)

Hand-coined canon (Joule Wars, Proof Debt, agent-hour…). Tiny but a good model for LEX-style editorial voice + RAG-grounding format (JSONL term/definition/coined_by/url).

9 — ConceptNet 5 (multilingual knowledge graph)

Words/phrases + common-sense relations (Synonym/Antonym/RelatedTo…) with weights, sourced from Wiktionary/WordNet/DBPedia. Use for connectsWith / related-terms edges in lexi_terms.

10 — Wiktionary structured (10.6M entries, 7 tables)

Flattened wiktextract: entries/senses/examples/translations/forms/sounds/linkages joined on entry_id. Best general fallback for definitions + synonyms/antonyms/hypernyms when OpenGloss lacks a tech term. CC-BY-SA share-alike.

11 — 3char (751k codes, 58 systems)

Registry of short codes people actually type: HTTP methods/status, CSS, MIME, git, errno, IATA/ICAO, etc., each with authority + regex. Fills the infra-acronym gap (API, HTTP, CSS) that AI sets miss. Check per-authority redistribution rights.

12 — en-wiktionary-sqlite-all (1.4M entries, lossless SQLite)

Fully normalized 40+-table SQLite: senses, glosses (1.75M), wikilinks (3.4M), translations, pronunciations, examples. Mobile/extension-friendly offline lexicon option.
