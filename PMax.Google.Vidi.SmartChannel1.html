What is Performance Max?

This section covers the fundamentals. Performance Max (PMax) is an AI-powered, goal-based campaign type that lets you access all Google Ads inventory from a single campaign. Think of it as an automation and amplification engine for the assets you're already creating.

The Core Engine: Your Inputs

PMax runs on two inputs: Assets (the "what") and Audience Signals (the "who"). This is where your AI-powered system directly plugs in. Click each to see how.

Assets: Your "Faceted Content"

These are the creative building blocks AI will mix and match. Your AI system is a perfect factory for these.

Text Assets

Your AI can generate dozens of headlines (e.g., "Save 20% Today," "Durable & Stylish") and descriptions for each content facet. PMax requires at least 5 headlines and 5 descriptions.

Image Assets

Provide a mix of images. Your AI-generated lifestyle shots, product close-ups, and infographics are all valuable. PMax will test them all to see what converts.

Video Assets

This is critical. Provide at least one video (10s+). Your AI-generated faceted videos are a huge advantage. If you don't provide one, PMax will auto-generate a low-quality one.

Signals: Your "Many Viewers"

These are not restrictive targeting. They are hints to tell the AI who to start looking for.

Your Data (First-Party)

Upload your customer lists, website visitor lists (e.g., "cart abandoners," "past purchasers"), and app users. This is the AI's #1 signal.

Custom Segments

Build audiences based on people who search for your keywords (e.g., "best trail running shoes") or browse specific types of websites (e.g., running blogs).

Google Audiences

Use Google's pre-built In-Market (e.g., "Apparel & Accessories") and Affinity (e.g., "Fitness Enthusiasts") audiences to give the AI a broader starting point.
