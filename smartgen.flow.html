Local Inference Hardware Strategy

Running top Vision Language Models locally requires significant VRAM. The RTX 4090 (24GB) handles Qwen3-VL 30B (Int4), MiniCPM-V, and CogVLM2 for production workloads, while dual RTX 6000 (96GB) unlocks InternVL3-78B at full precision.

SmartGen Tech Stack Composition

A balanced ecosystem relying heavily on **Orchestration** (n8n, LangGraph) and **Data Infrastructure** (VidiSmart, Neo4j) to support the AI models.

Agentic Workflow: The "Brain"

Directus Flows & n8n

Acts as the central nervous system. Webhooks from Directus trigger n8n workflows that dispatch tasks to specific AI agents.

LangGraph & CrewAI

Manages stateful, multi-turn conversations. Agents like "Researcher" (Crawl4AI) and "Writer" (Qwen 3) collaborate to verify facts.

LiveKit & Tavus

Real-time communication interfaces. Tavus generates personalized video responses based on CRM data in Directus.

GraphRAG (Neo4j)

Connects unstructured vector data with structured relationship graphs, enabling "Smart Search" (Swirl AI) to find hidden links.

AI-Driven Analytics
