PULSING EFFECTS

CSS-based pulsing and breathing effects for status indicators. These are lightweight alternatives to WebGL for simpler visual feedback.

  • • Breathing Circle - scale + opacity pulse
  • • Pulsing Ring - expanding ring effect
  • • Orbiting Dots - circular motion
  • • Wave Bars - audio visualizer style
  • • Glowing Dot - box-shadow glow
  • • Spinning Cross - rotation animation
