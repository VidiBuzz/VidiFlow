Why This Rank

Program trading — the same systematic, algorithmic strategies that Wall Street firms deploy with billion-dollar infrastructure — has never been accessible to the average retail investor. VidiFund changes that. The 20-agent swarm architecture (born from the Fortuna Trade project) coordinates across equities, options, futures, and crypto simultaneously. The investor-matching engine pairs each user with strategies suited to their profile. Combined moat: proprietary agent architecture + real-time execution infrastructure + performance data flywheel that improves with every trade.

Competition

20+ AI quant funds exist, but none offer a 20-agent swarm architecture to retail investors. Robo-advisors (Betterment, Wealthfront) pre-date multimodal AI and offer passive index strategies only. Retail trading platforms (Robinhood, E*Trade) provide tools but no autonomous execution. VidiFund is the first to bring institutional-grade program trading to the individual investor.
