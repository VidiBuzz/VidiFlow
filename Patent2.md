### ❌ What You CANNOT Patent (Under Current Law)

| Cannot Be Patented | Why |
|---|---|
| **Religious beliefs/practices themselves** | Protected by First Amendment; not subject matter eligible for patents |
| **Scientific laws/natural phenomena** | Explicitly excluded from patent eligibility (35 U.S.C. §101) |
| **Universal interpretations of physics** | Abstract ideas and scientific principles cannot be patented alone |
| **Anything covering "all religions" or "all sciences"** | This is not a specific, concrete invention - it's too broad and abstract |

### ✅ What CAN Be Patented

You can patent a **specific technological application** that uses religious or scientific frameworks as a foundation, but the patent must claim:
- A particular device, machine, process, or composition
- Concrete technical implementation with measurable utility
- Novel features that are not obvious to experts in the field

## 📋 PROVISIONAL PATENT APPLICATION - ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS (2025 Requirements)

To make a **successful submission**, your provisional patent application MUST include:

| Element | Required Content |
|---|---|
| **1. Cover Sheet** | Form PTO/SB/16 with inventor names, title, correspondence address |
| **2. Written Description** | Complete enablement - someone skilled in the art can reproduce it (10-15+ pages) |
| **3. Technical Drawings/Figures** | Diagrams showing how invention works (essential for clarity) |
| **4. Best Mode Disclosure** | How you would actually implement the invention |
| **5. Filing Fee** | $65 (micro entity), $130 (small entity), or $325 (large entity) as of 2025 |

### ✅ Checklist for Your Application:

- [ ] Clear, specific title describing the actual invention (not "universal system")
- [ ] Background section explaining current limitations/problems
- [ ] Summary of your novel solution
- [ ] Detailed technical description with specifications, measurements, materials
- [ ] Flowcharts/algorithms if software-based
- [ ] Embodiments/examples showing practical implementation
- [ ] Advantages and benefits over existing technology
- [ ] **NOT** broad philosophical or religious statements
