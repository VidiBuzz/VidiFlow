Terminology, decoded

So—are any of them omni?

01

Multimodal

More than text—usually text plus images.

02

Omni-input

Understands text, images, video, and audio in one model.

03

Full omni

Also generates images, audio, and video natively. None here qualifies.

✓

Omni-input

Gemini 3.8 Flash · MiMo-V2.6-Flash · Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor

Each accepts text, images, video, and audio. All three still return text only.

×

Full omni-output

None of the five

No model in the roster natively generates image, speech, music, or video output.

Cline caveat: provider capability does not guarantee interface support. The model may accept audio or video while Cline only forwards text and images. Verify the selected provider’s attachment pipeline before assuming the full modality matrix is available.
