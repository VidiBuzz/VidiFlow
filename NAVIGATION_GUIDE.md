## 📝 How to Add Navigation to a Page

Copy the content from `unified-nav.html` and paste it right after the `<body>` tag:

```html
<body>
    <!-- Paste unified-nav.html content here -->

    <!-- Rest of your page content -->
</body>
```

The navigation will automatically:
1. Stick to the top
2. Highlight the current page
3. Show mobile menu on small screens
4. Display contact info on desktop
