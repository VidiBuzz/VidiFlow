📊 Executive Summary

The agent orchestration landscape fractured in early 2026 after OpenClaw became GitHub's fastest-growing open source project (now 376K stars). This created a security crisis that led to enterprise-focused alternatives: NVIDIA's NemoClaw, OpenFang (Rust-based security-first OS), and ZeroClaw (ultra-lightweight Rust alternative). New entrants Hermes Agent (177K stars, Kanban multi-agent swarm) and the LangChain Stack (LangGraph + Deep Agents) have expanded the ecosystem to 10 major platforms. This comparison analyzes the top 10 platforms across security, performance, channels, and enterprise readiness.
