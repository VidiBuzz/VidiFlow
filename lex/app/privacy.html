Lexi — Privacy Policy

VidiSmart · last updated 28 September 2026 · applies to the Lexi Chrome extension, the Lexi Android app, and the Lex web app at vidiflow.net/lex.

Short version: Lexi does not collect, sell, rent or transmit your personal data. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, and no user account is required to use it.

1. What Lexi reads

  • Browser extension: the text of the web page you are looking at, inside your browser tab, to highlight known tech terms. That text is processed in the page and is never transmitted anywhere and never stored.
  • Android app: only the text you explicitly select and hand to Lexi from another app (the “Lexi — decode this term” menu item), or text you paste into the app yourself.
  • Web app: nothing. The Lex web app runs entirely in your browser.

2. What Lexi stores (on your device only)

  • Smart Stack: the terms you choose to save, plus any note you write. Stored in your browser’s local extension storage, or in the Android app’s private app storage.
  • Preferences: highlight on/off, minimum term length, case sensitivity, and any sites you have muted.
  • That data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (the study package / JSON backup buttons).
  • Erase everything any time: the extension options page has “Delete all my data”, the Android app has “Delete all” in the Stack tab, and clearing browser site data removes the extension’s copy.

3. Network requests Lexi makes

  • Dictionary updates (extension): a request to https://cdn.vidi.news/lexi/terms.json to keep the term list current. It carries no user identifier, no cookies and none of your saved terms. As with any web request, the CDN provider processes the request’s IP address for delivery and security — standard hosting behaviour, not data collection by Lexi.
  • Concept images (extension and Android): https://cdn.vidi.news/images/lexi/<ACR>.jpg, fetched only when you open a term’s detail view.
  • Web app: loads Lexi/Max avatar artwork from the same CDN.
  • Lexi makes no other network requests, contacts no analytics or advertising services, and sends nothing to VidiSmart servers.

4. What Lexi never does

  • No reading of page content from outside your active tab.
  • No keylogging, no screen capture, no microphone or camera access.
  • On Android, no background clipboard scraping: clipboard text is read only when you tap Lexi yourself.
  • No selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Permissions, and why each one is needed

  • Extension — “read and change your data on all sites”: required to highlight acronyms on the pages you visit. The extension injects only its own highlight spans and tooltip, and you can mute any site.
  • Extension — “storage”: to save your Smart Stack and preferences on your device.
  • Extension — network access to cdn.vidi.news: to download dictionary updates and concept images. It cannot reach any other host.
  • Android — “Process text” / share intent: so Lexi appears in the text-selection menu and share sheet of your other apps.
  • Android — Internet: reserved for optional dictionary updates in a future version. Version 0.1 works fully offline.

6. Children

Lexi is a business/productivity tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this same URL with a new “last updated” date, and material changes are noted in the extension’s and the app’s release notes.
