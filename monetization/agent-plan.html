📊 Executive Dashboard & Automated Weekly Briefings
1
Market Research Agent

Continuously searches SEC filings, Crunchbase, patent databases, and government registries to validate market claims, TAM sizing, and competitive landscapes.

Key Responsibilities
  • Validate TAM / SAM / SOM across each monetization plan
  • Identify emerging competitors and track funding rounds
  • Cross-reference federal registries (SAM.gov, ACE, CBP)
  • Track recent M&A and IPO activity in core verticals
2
Financial Modeling Agent

Builds dynamic revenue projections, CapEx models, and break-even timelines using comparable SaaS benchmarks and live public market multiples.

Key Responsibilities
  • Build 3-scenario revenue models (Conservative / Base / Optimistic)
  • Calculate unit economics, blended margins, and LTV/CAC ratios
  • Model CapEx requirements with structured contingency buffers
  • Generate investor-grade automated financial projections
3
Technical Architecture Agent

Evaluates tech stack requirements, identifies shared infrastructure reuse across plans, and estimates engineering complexity and GPU compute budgets.

Key Responsibilities
  • Map shared infrastructure (Vespa vector, Directus CMS, R2 CDN, SmartGen)
  • Estimate engineering sprint effort per architectural layer
  • Design API-first modular architectures for licensing
  • Benchmark GPU token consumption & inference latency
4
Competitive Intelligence Agent

Monitors competitor product updates, feature rollouts, pricing shifts, and strategic positioning across all active market segments.

Key Responsibilities
  • Track competitor changelogs and product roadmap shifts
  • Monitor SaaS pricing, packaging tiers, and discounts
  • Identify high-value enterprise partnership opportunities
  • Deliver structured monthly competitive differentiation matrices
5
Regulatory & Compliance Agent

Tracks regulatory updates, filing requirements, and compliance deadlines relevant to automated trading, tariff filings, and federal procurement contracts.

Key Responsibilities
  • Monitor SCOTUS and trade policy rulings in real time
  • Track CBP ACE system updates and EDI/REST API specs
  • Audit SEC/FINRA compliance guidelines for algorithmic models
  • Maintain and verify SAM.gov active registration state
6
Synthesis & Reporting Agent

Aggregates multi-agent intelligence streams into executive summaries, weekly briefing decks, and automated real-time KPI dashboards.

Key Responsibilities
  • Generate automated weekly executive briefing reports
  • Assemble monthly investor and board presentations
  • Maintain live telemetry sync with internal KPI dashboards
  • Flag critical operational risks and strategic inflection points
