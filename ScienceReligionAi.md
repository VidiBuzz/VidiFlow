## ⚖️ CRITICAL PATENT INFORMATION

⚠️ **IMPORTANT: You CANNOT patent religions or scientific principles themselves.** 

According to current U.S. and international patent law (2025):

### ❌ What Cannot Be Patented:
- Religious beliefs, practices, or doctrines
- Scientific theories, laws of nature, or natural phenomena
- Abstract ideas or mathematical formulas alone
- Pure interpretations of physics or other sciences

### ✅ What CAN Be Patented:
- **Specific applications** of scientific principles
- Novel devices, machines, processes, or compositions
- Practical implementations with concrete utility
- Technical solutions to specific problems

## 📋 PROVISIONAL PATENT APPLICATION GUIDELINES (2025)

### Essential Elements for a Successful Submission:

1. **Title**: Clear and descriptive of the invention
2. **Field of Invention**: What technical area it belongs to
3. **Background**: Current state-of-the-art and problems being solved
4. **Summary**: Brief overview of what you've invented
5. **Detailed Description**: Complete explanation enabling someone skilled in the art to reproduce it
6. **Drawings/Figures**: Technical diagrams (if applicable)
7. **Claims** (for non-provisional): Specific scope of protection sought

### Key Requirements:
- **Novelty**: Must be new and not previously disclosed
- **Utility**: Must have a practical use
- **Non-obviousness**: Not an obvious improvement to experts in the field
