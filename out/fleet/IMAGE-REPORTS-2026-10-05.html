Your Gemini complaint is explained, not contradicted. Both benchmarks put Nano Banana in the passing tier — but both used short, quoted strings with an explicit “render exactly” instruction. Real page copy is longer and multi-line. That is why the benchmarks pass and your pages don’t. Keep it for illustration, not typography.
Two things worth knowing before you build on this
Latin copy is close to solved. CJK, diacritics and small print are not. SandBase’s vision-LLM grader silently “corrected” 14 Chinese strings — if you auto-verify CJK with a vision model it will lie. A human has to read those.

FLUX.2 ranks #1 of 23 at IMG.LY but 6/10 at SandBase, and BFL markets it as best-in-class for text. The contradiction is real — FLUX is an English-only lane.

Recommended next step

Pull HiDream-O1 + Mage-Flow (~12.3 GB total, both MIT), then run a character-exact transcription test on real VidiFlow brand strings before trusting either. Six prompts separates 0.91 from 0.87.
