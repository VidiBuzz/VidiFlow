23D Visualization Models

These render, segment, and reason over 3D volumes. NVIDIA-family unless noted.

3D vision-language

NV-Reason-CT

3D VLM for chest/abdominal CT. Report generation, visual Q&A, chain-of-thought reasoning over full volumes.

Score: macro-F1 0.614, macro-AUROC 0.871 on CT-RATE. Trained on ~550K QA pairs from 70,111 CT volumes. NIfTI input.

3D generation

NV-Generate-CT

3D latent diffusion for synthetic CT generation — augmentation and rare-condition synthesis.

research only not a clinical product. Up to 512×512×768 voxels, 127 anatomical classes with paired masks.

3D generation

NV-Generate-MR

3D latent diffusion for synthetic MR — T1/FLAIR/T2/SWI with optional anatomical annotations. 240M params, 3D UNet + attention, MONAI.

non-commercial NVIDIA OneWay licence — academic research only.

3D contrastive

CT-CLIP

3D contrastive vision-language pretraining on chest CT. Self-supervised multi-abnormality detection and case retrieval. A100 80GB recommended for training. 3D ViT.

3D vision-language

RadViLLA

3D VLM radiology across chest, abdomen, pelvis. Mount Sinai + NVIDIA. 75K 3D CT scans, 1M+ VQA pairs.

3D chat

CT-CHAT

3D chest-CT vision-language chat. 2.7M+ VQA pairs from CT-RATE. CT-CLIP vision encoder + pretrained LLM.

MONAI model zoo

vista3d, brats_mri_segmentation, brain_image_synthesis_latent_diffusion_model, renalStructures_UNEST_segmentation, wholeBrainSeg_Large_UNEST_segmentation, retinalOCT_RPD_segmentation. Broadest coverage.

Segmentation

MedSAM2

3Integration Feasibility

Medical NLP in workflow: MEDIUM. AntAngelMed works as the medical-reasoning layer — ingesting clinical notes, synthesizing capacity/occupancy reports, triaging bed/resource requests, writing structured summaries for VidiFlow nodes.

Direct 3D visualization: LOW. AntAngelMed has no imaging head. Cannot render, segment, or reason over 3D volumes. Not a drop-in for imaging models.

Practical path: Reasoning layer: AntAngelMed served via vLLM/SGLang or hosted API. Visualization layer: NVIDIA 3D models (NV-Reason-CT, CT-CLIP, RadViLLA, NV-Generate-CT) via Hugging Face transformers. Embeddings: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B + Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B (already in stack; VL handles image/video frames natively).
